## Summary

In February 2025, Jess, Ada, Eris and I moved from 24550 Miller Hill Rd Los Gatos, California to 6307 Melvick Pt Colorado Springs, Colorado. 80927 This document serves as a ledger of what we did, what worked, and what didn't.

## What We Did

### Packing

#### Boxing

Will started boxing his games early, then focused on puzzles, comics, and technology-centric areas like the desks and the media centers (2 TVs and the Record Player). 

Jess packed the kitchen, focusing on what we couldn't replace, the bathroom, her makeup area, her books, and the pet stuff. 

Each of us took care of our own wardrobe.

#### Packing the Trailer

We rented the largest U-Haul trailer we could. The trailer itself weighted 1920 lbs, and [[Shiela]] has a turbocharged engine that allows her to tow a total of 3500 lbs, leaving us with about 1580 lbs worth of luggage. 

The original rental date for the trailer was Monday 2/17 to Friday 2/21. We originally planned to pick it up and pack 2/17, leave the 18th, arrive the 20th and return the trailer on the 21st. Ultimately, we weren't done loading the trailer until the 21st. We left the 22nd, arrived in the latter half of the 24th, then unloaded and returned the trailer on the 25th. Our late fees were about $90. 

We had a lot of help from [[Abel Plachno|Abe]] to fill up the trailer. We had a loading zone outside of the trailer where Jess would move things so that Abe and Will could see what was heavier and what was bigger. 

As we loaded the trailer, we decided to section off the heaviest things which we filled to the ceiling of the trailer. We covered it with a furniture rug, then used cables against the rug to keep things in place. 

### Moving

We had the cats in the back of the car and drove for 6-8 hours a day, stopping every two or three hours. Will drove the entire time. 

While we had a hotel we planned to end the day at, we kept it open. The first night, we were tired and had to change plans on how far we would get. Our second night, in Albuquerque, we chose to stay at Motel 6 Studios, Motel 6's extended stay. 

### Unpacking

After resting for the night, we got up early and unloaded the trailer. While we simply started at the front and worked our way through, we did generally separate things in two dimensions: what room would they belong to, and will we need them soon or can they simply be stored until we have the time and attention to make sure they are properly dealt with.

We were able to fully unload the trailer in about 2 hours, and return the trailer to the U-Haul place 2 hours later (after a shower for each of us). 

For the next couple days, we would try to approach a "room" at a time, always doing at least one small thing, sometimes having the energy to do quite a few. 

## What Worked

### Packing

#### Boxing

Boxing things as early as possible gives you a sense of exactly how much work the move is going to take. 

Having one person do all of a category helped to keep one person as lead for during unpacking. 

Focusing on what we needed and couldn't replace helped keep the amount we were bringing low.

#### Packing the Trailer

The combo of the [[Shiela|Outback]] and the trailer was perfect. 

Having a loading zone close to the trailer for things ready to get put in helped organize things pretty well.

Separating the trailer load into segments did help keep things from getting damaged over the trip. 

### Moving

Will was originally worried that driving 6 hours with the cats unable to stop and use the bathroom would be a problem, but apparently the girls have better control over the waste management than Will does. 

Stopping every couple hours helped break the trip into manageable chunks and gave us a good cycle for breaks. 

Having a hotel already planned for gave us a hard stopping point that was much appreciated. Once you are in the car, it is easy to decide to just keep going, even though that may not be the best choice. 

### Unpacking

This went pretty well: unpacking directly into the storage area, then moving necessary, room-centric boxes to where they go made the unpacking flow pretty smoothly. 

The quicker you unload, the quicker you can get the rental back and not get too overcharged for being late. That said, take your time... it is only $22 or so for each day late.
