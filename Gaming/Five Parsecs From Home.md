**Five Parsecs From Home** is a solitaire roleplaying wargame by Modiphius Entertainment. All references on this page are specifically to the third edition of the game, released in [[Five Parsecs From Home Third Edition |hardback form]] in 2021.

There are currently three releases in this line:
- [[Five Parsecs From Home Third Edition]] (2021) - The Core Book
- [[Five Parsecs From Home Compendium]] (2023) - The Expansion Modules
- [[Five Parsecs From Home Tactics]] (2024) - The Warfare Ruleset

## The Ruleset

Five Parsecs From Home sees players creating a [[Crew (5PFH) |crew]] of science-fiction mercenaries in a futuristic dystopia and sending them on contracts, collecting [[Rumor (5PFH) |rumors]] to find [[Quest (5PFH) |quests]], fighting [[Enemies (5PFH) |gangsters and beasts]], and even developing [[Rival (5PFH) |rivals]]. This occurs in a cycle of [[Campaign Turn (5PFH) |turns]] that culminate in a full [[Campaign (5PFH) |campaign]].

To play, you will [[Tools & Materials (5PFH) |need some materials]], including some dice, some miniatures, and some way to record your campaign. You may want to use a [[Crew Tracking (5PFH)|crew sheet]] to track your [[Crew Member (5PFH) |crew members]] and their [[Resources (5PFH)|resources]].
